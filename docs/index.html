(04) The panel, tab by tab

Your day.
One hover.

Pin keeps the panel open while you work. Optional features are switched on in Settings → Features, so the panel only shows what you use.

01

Today

Your agenda for the day, with Join on any event that has a Zoom, Meet, Teams or Lark link, and the service each calendar comes from beside the time.

Google · Lark · iCloud
02

Clipboard

The last 200 things you copied, opened at the text cursor. Pick one and it's pasted.

⇧⌘V for easy clip
03

Notes

A rich-text note per day, stamped with the time you wrote each line.

Covelet Notes
04

Drop

Files and recent screenshots wait in the notch, in stacks. Pick several and send them by AirDrop, LocalSend or a browser page on your phone.

AirDrop · LocalSend · QR
05

Music

Controls for whatever is playing, from Spotify and Music to YouTube in a browser. Lyrics scroll karaoke style, with a small equalizer in the bar.

Spotify · Music · YouTube
06

Pomodoro

A focus timer in the bar: green while you work, amber on a short break, red on a long one.

Lives in the bar
07

Device

CPU, memory and battery at a glance, plus Keep awake for long downloads.

Keep awake
08

World Clock

An analog clock card for each city your team lives in. Step every clock an hour at a time to find a time that suits everyone.

Time zones
09

AI usage

Tokens and cost from your own logs, Claude's live quota if you switch it on, and a notice from the notch when an agent finishes or needs you.

Claude Code · Codex · Your favorite provider
(05) What leaves your Mac

Nothing leaves your Mac.

No account, no analytics. Your calendars, clipboard and files stay on this Mac, with clipboard history encrypted in your Keychain. Covelet only goes online to look up lyrics, check for updates and, if you switch it on, fetch your Claude quota.

No sign-upOpen it and it works. There's nothing to log in to.
Encrypted historyClipboard history is sealed with a key kept in your Keychain.
Your Wi-Fi onlyFiles go straight to your own devices. No hassle.
