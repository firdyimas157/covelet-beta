(04) The panel, tab by tab

Your day.
One hover.

Pin keeps the panel open while you work. Optional features are switched on in Settings → Features, so the panel only shows what you use.

01

Today

Your agenda for the day, with Join on any Zoom, Meet, Teams or Lark call and a reminder 5, 10 or 15 minutes before. Press + to add an event hour by hour, saved to Google, Lark or iCloud.

Google · Lark · iCloud
02

Clipboard

The last 200 things you copied, opened at the text cursor. Pick one and it's pasted.

⇧⌘V for easy clip
03

Notes

A rich-text note per day, stamped with the time you wrote each line.

Covelet Notes
04

Drop

Files and recent screenshots wait in the notch, in stacks. Pick several and send them by AirDrop, LocalSend or a browser page on your phone, encrypted on the way.

AirDrop · LocalSend · QR
05

Music

Controls for whatever is playing, from Spotify and Music to YouTube in a browser. Lyrics scroll karaoke style, with a small equalizer in the bar.

Spotify · Music · YouTube
06

Pomodoro

A focus timer in the bar: green while you work, amber on a short break, red on a long one.

Lives in the bar
07

Device

CPU, memory and battery at a glance, a volume for each app and each browser tab, and Keep awake for long downloads.

Per-app volume
08

World Clock

An analog clock card for each city your team lives in, with the weather there. Step every clock an hour at a time to find a time that suits everyone.

Time zones · Weather
09

Selfie

A quick camera in the notch, with effects and a timer. Photos land in Drop, ready to send.

Effects · Timer
10

AI usage

Tokens and cost from your own logs, Claude's live quota if you switch it on, and a notice from the notch when an agent finishes or needs you.

Claude Code · Codex · Your favorite provider
11

The notch itself

Volume and brightness levels, charger and headphone cards, and a warning at 20% and 10% battery. On an external display, it follows you with a notch of its own.

Always there

(05) What leaves your Mac

Nothing leaves your Mac.

No account, no analytics. Your calendars, clipboard and files stay on this Mac, with clipboard history encrypted in your Keychain. Covelet only goes online to look up lyrics and the weather, check for updates and, if you switch it on, fetch your Claude quota. Each permission is asked the first time you use its feature, never up front.

No sign-upOpen it and it works. There's nothing to log in to.
Encrypted historyClipboard history is sealed with a key kept in your Keychain.
Your Wi-Fi onlyFiles go straight to your own devices, encrypted on the way.

Install

Install Covelet in a minute.

The third step only happens the first time: Covelet is a free indie beta and isn't notarized by Apple yet. After that, updates install themselves from the notch.

  1. Covelet 0.1.8
    →
    Step 1

    Download and open the DMG

    Open Covelet-0.1.8.dmg from Downloads. A window shows Covelet and your Applications folder.

  2. Applications
    Step 2

    Drag it into Applications

    Drag Covelet onto Applications, then open it from there. Replacing an older copy keeps your notes and settings.

  3. “Covelet” Not OpenedApple could not verify “Covelet”…DoneMove to Trash
    Privacy & Security
    Security
    App Store & Known Developers
    “Covelet” was blocked.Open Anyway
    Step 3

    Blocked? Click Open Anyway

    If macOS says Covelet can't be verified, click Done. In System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to Security, click Open Anyway, confirm, then Open.

    Only once — updates don't ask again
  4. Welcome to CoveletRest the pointer on the notch to open it.Continue
    Step 4

    Say hello from the notch

    A welcome card drops down; nothing to allow yet. Covelet asks only when you first use something: Calendar from Today, Accessibility on your first paste.

Download for macOS To uninstall: switch off Launch at login, quit from the Settings tab, and drag Covelet to the Trash.
